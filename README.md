# next-smart-pos
AI Smart POS is an enterprise-grade SaaS POS platform for restaurants and retail businesses with multi-location management, ordering, modifiers, meal deals, terminals, staff control, analytics, reporting, and real-time operational workflows.

## 🚀 Overview

AI Smart POS is a modern enterprise-level POS ecosystem designed for restaurants, retail chains, cafés, and multi-branch businesses.

The platform focuses on:

- Multi-location management
- Advanced ordering workflows
- Enterprise dashboard experience
- Smart product & modifier system
- Reporting & analytics
- Terminal management
- Staff & permission control
- Scalable SaaS architecture

Currently, the project is focused on **high-fidelity frontend UI development** while backend architecture and business logic are under active development.
